🖼️ Wander

A 3D gallery of real, openly-licensed art. Walk up to a picture and the room becomes it.

WASD to move · drag to look · arrow keys to turn · E/click to step into a picture

· · source

Every piece is real: openly-licensed art and photography pulled live from Openverse, texture-mapped onto the gallery walls — never generated by a model. A picture that will not load still gets a place on the wall, colored and labelled from its own title. Step into any picture and the whole room's walls, fog and light tween to that picture's own colors. Source.
